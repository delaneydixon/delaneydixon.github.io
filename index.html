How I work

Something is unclear, broken or not mature enough. I work through it the same way each time.

  1. Diagnose
    Figure out what is actually wrong, and why
  2. Frame
    Turn an ambiguous problem into a structured problem statement
  3. Design
    Develop future-state processes, operating models, requirements or solution options
  4. Align
    Bring Business, Operations, Product and Technology to the same page
  5. Translate
    Turn business needs into requirements, recommendations and actionable work
  6. Drive
    Own scope, stakeholders, risks, dependencies and delivery
  7. Measure
    Use KPIs, business cases and performance data to show what changed
  8. Improve
    Iterate on feedback and what implementation teaches
